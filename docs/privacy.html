YOUR MUSIC, YOUR DEVICE

Privacy & rights

Understand what stays here, when Wave connects, and the controls you have.

Updated 29 October 2026 · Version 0.1.1

Wave is local-first, not a backup service: keep your original music files. Public deployment operators are responsible for their own legal identity and privacy contact.

Your library stays on your device

Music, metadata, favorites, playlists, images and cached lyrics are kept in this browser's IndexedDB; preferences and the playback session use localStorage. No accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud synchronization or audio uploads are implemented. Clearing site data, private browsing or storage eviction can erase the stored copies. Original files on your disk are not changed or deleted.

Queue, progress and appearance

Your selected song, queue order, repeat/shuffle and playback positions are saved in this browser. Reloading restores a paused session, never automatic playback. This is a current-session bookmark, not an account or a cloud backup. It is scoped to this website origin; a different browser, device or deployment address has separate storage.

Artwork colors are sampled locally to style the interface. Automatic playlist icons combine local cover colors in memory; they do not send images to a design service or modify your audio. Theme and the last palette can be remembered locally so this notice matches Wave.

Your controls

  • Keep your originals. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. Wave is not a backup.
  • Remove one song or playlist. Use its options in the library. Removing a playlist does not delete the music.
  • Remove downloaded lyrics. Open the lyric editor. Manual lyrics take precedence over online matches.
  • Clear everything for this site. Use your browser’s site-data controls. This removes local copies, artwork, preferences and saved progress; it cannot erase the original files on disk or copies held elsewhere.

Online lyrics are optional

Automatic lyrics is off initially. Enabling it in Settings sends song title, artist, optional album/duration and a client identifier to LRCLIB. The provider also receives connection information such as your IP address. Audio and artwork are not sent. The Search online action requests one song even while automatic lookup is off. Turn automatic lookup off to stop new automatic requests; an already sent request cannot be recalled. Saved text remains local and can be removed in the lyric editor.

LRCLIB documentation describes API use. Provider privacy/retention terms and a blanket copyright license for lyrics were not verified during review. API availability does not grant rights to copyrighted text.

Metadata checks stay on your device

Imports inspect tags, duration and embedded covers locally, using a same-origin worker where supported. Original audio stays unchanged. Local checks do not identify recordings against an external catalogue.

Online catalogue checks and cover downloading have been removed. Imports and metadata review make no MusicBrainz or Cover Art Archive requests, including when an older verification preference is saved. Previously saved details and covers remain local; they are not automatically erased or reverted. Prior provider requests cannot be recalled. Use local editing/restoration or browser site-data controls to manage saved data. The separate optional lyric-search policy is unchanged.

Local continuous listening

Autoplay selects only from your local songs, using artist, album, available genre/year, favorites, duration and recent-session IDs with weighted variety. No online recommendation profile or streaming audio is requested. Repeat modes take priority; Autoplay can be switched off.

Mix works on the device. Wave analyses a downmixed copy of two songs while they play and plans the handover in memory; nothing is uploaded. During an overlap only the playback changes: the two decks are faded against each other, the low end changes hands, the outgoing song loses its top and the incoming one may be tempo-matched with pitch preserved. All of it is reverted when the overlap ends, and the stored file, its sample rate, its bit depth and its bitrate are never touched.

Video clips and songs from YouTube

Clips. To find the video behind a song, Wave sends only the song title and artist to public YouTube front-ends (Piped and Invidious instances) and keeps the chosen video id in this browser. Those servers also receive connection information such as your IP address. No audio, artwork or library list leaves the device, and a clip is only ever searched for the song you are playing. You can remove a remembered clip from that song's options.

Songs added from YouTube. Wave stores the video id, not a file. The title and length are read from YouTube's oEmbed endpoint; when that request is blocked by the browser, noembed, a Piped instance or an Invidious instance is tried instead, carrying the video id and the usual connection information. Nothing about your library is included.

Playback. A YouTube song, and the muted clip behind Now Playing, stream directly from YouTube inside your browser. That traffic, and the fact that a video was watched, belongs to YouTube's own policy rather than to Wave. Wave does not download, proxy, extract or re-encode that audio, and a song you also imported as a file always plays from your copy instead.

Word-by-word lyric sources are optional

Word-by-word highlighting works with the lyrics Wave already has. Two extra community sources that publish word timings — NetEase and Kugou — are asked only if you turn on Word-level lyric sources in Settings, which is off initially. Only the song title and artist are sent; both services see connection information such as your IP address and neither publishes a stable API contract. Their answers are parsed on the device and saved as ordinary lyrics. Turning the switch off stops new requests; lyrics already saved stay available offline.

Offline copy of the interface

The web edition registers a service worker that stores the interface — HTML, scripts, styles, fonts and icons — so Wave opens without a connection. Audio, artwork, lyrics and video references are never added to that cache; they stay in the library storage described above. Unregistering the service worker or clearing site data removes the offline copy, and the next visit needs a connection.

The website host

Visiting a GitHub Pages deployment downloads the client from GitHub, which may process IP/device/request information under its privacy statement. Other hosts have their own policies. Fonts are served with Wave, not fetched from a font CDN.

Music, lyrics and artwork rights

Use only content you are entitled to access and use. Free/open-source software does not authorize copying or distributing music, lyrics or covers. Wave bundles no music catalogue, bypasses no access controls and does not download streaming audio. Spain's private-copy exception has specific cumulative conditions; it is not permission to download arbitrary online material. This is not legal advice or certification.

Contact and further information

Read the technical privacy notice, and project source. Public issues are visible to others: do not attach personal music, private metadata or credentials. Applicable privacy rights and complaint channels must be explained by the actual deployment operator.
