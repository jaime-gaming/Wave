# Wave 0.2.1

**Release date: 1 November 2026.** The web client is published at
[wave.jaime-gaming.com](https://wave.jaime-gaming.com). This release is about how Wave looks
and about what it is willing to claim: every icon is drawn in the app's own line, the wordmark
is finished, a clip has room on the screen, and the app stops calling things wrong when they
are not.

## The drawings

- The icon pack is gone. Navigation, transport, rows, cards and dialogs are drawn in one file
  against one grid, with one stroke weight and round caps, the way the mark is drawn.
- The library is a stack of covers, settings is a mixer, pause is two capsules rather than two
  outlined boxes that smudged at small sizes.
- The `v` in **Wave** is rebuilt on the W's own trough: both arms carry the same weight and the
  letter stands on the baseline instead of leaning into a hinge.

## A clip has room

- With a video playing, each part of the player has its own place in the grid, so nothing sits
  on top of anything else: the sleeve, the title and the transport in the left column, the
  words in the right one, and the picture in the middle.
- The picture keeps almost all of itself; a heavy crop was what made a wide video look cut off.
- A missing clip is mentioned in a small note beside the transport, not across the middle.
- During a mix, the arriving song's clip fades in on the same progress number as its sound.

## Fewer wrong claims

- A title is called a filename only when it plainly is one. Titles containing "Take", "Mix",
  "Copy" or "Final" used to be renamed by the catalogue.
- "The artist and the title look swapped" is only said when the file name itself holds them the
  other way round.
- A comma names two acts; a slash does not, so `AC/DC` is one artist again.
- Silence has to reach three seconds at the front or four at the back before Wave mentions it,
  and a missing year or genre is no longer treated as a gap.

## Still open

- Android and Windows cannot link to each other or to the web yet. The web pairing stack exists;
  the native apps have no transport at all.
